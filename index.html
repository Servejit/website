01 / ABOUT

Curiosity for markets. Clarity through data.

My interests sit at the intersection of financial markets, technical analysis and data analytics. I enjoy studying how price behaves, exploring repeatable market conditions and building tools that make research easier to understand.

My work is practical and iterative: define a question, organise the data, test the idea and present the result clearly. I am especially interested in Indian equities and systematic market research.

04 / MY APPROACH

From question to insight.

A simple process keeps analysis transparent, repeatable and easier to review.

01 / DEFINE

Ask the right question

Start with a clear market or data question and define the conditions to examine.

02 / COLLECT

Structure the data

Gather relevant information and organise it for consistent analysis.

03 / TEST

Evaluate the idea

Check historical behaviour and examine limitations instead of assuming an outcome.

04 / COMMUNICATE

Make it understandable

Present findings with clear visuals, context and transparent assumptions.
